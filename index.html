About this stream

Each track picks a random riwayah (a canonically transmitted reading of the Qur'an, such as Hafs 'an 'Asim or Warsh 'an Nafi') and then a random reciter who has recorded that riwayah, streaming directly from mp3quran.net's public library. When a surah ends, it automatically moves on to a new random combination.

Tip: open this page in your phone's browser and use "Add to Home Screen" so it behaves like an app and keeps playing with your screen locked.
